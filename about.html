Who We Are

Fostering Brotherhood. Elevating Our Alma Mater.

The CIC Alumni 1995 Set is the official fellowship uniting graduates of the prestigious College of the Immaculate Conception (Class of 1995) across Nigeria and the global diaspora.

Since walking out through the historic gates of CIC Enugu in 1995, our members have distinguished themselves globally in medicine, engineering, business, law, academia, civil service, and technology. United by our shared formative years, we come together to ensure that no member walks alone, while continuing to give back generously to the institution that shaped us.

Our Vision

To build a vibrant, empowered, and unified alumni network that champions member welfare, professional excellence, and institutional greatness for CIC Enugu.

Our Mission

To connect former students, nurture brotherhood, empower members economically, and invest in modern campus infrastructure, scholarships, and STEM laboratories.

Our Core Pillars & Objectives:

  • Fraternal Solidarity: Strengthen lifelong fraternity and professional mentorship among 1995 classmates worldwide.
  • Welfare & Healthcare Shield: Provide swift emergency financial assistance, health aid, and celebratory support during key life events.
  • Campus Infrastructure: Modernize academic facilities, digital libraries, ICT hubs, and renewable energy on the CIC Enugu campus.
  • Indigent Scholarships: Sponsor promising secondary school students and Seminarians facing severe financial hardship.

Fraternal Covenant

Membership in CIC Alumni 1995 Set

Rooted in our timeless Latin motto "Semper Fidelis" (Always Faithful), membership is open to every student who completed or attended secondary school education with the 1995 set at CIC Enugu.

Welfare & Healthcare Shield

Active members enjoy immediate fraternal distress coverage, subsidized medical emergency funds, and solidarity support during weddings, births, and bereavement.

Global Chapter Fellowships

Participate in monthly chapter assemblies across Enugu Central, Lagos Main, Abuja FCT, Port Harcourt, UK Diaspora, and USA/North America.

Alma Mater Stewardship

Directly shape student life at CIC Enugu by financing science laboratories, ICT technology centres, sports pavilions, and indigent student scholarships.

Audited Digital Receipts

100% financial transparency. Receive tamper-proof digital receipts for all statutory dues and levies, verifiable instantly on the public verification portal.
